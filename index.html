About Krishi Mitra Assam

Connecting farmers with useful agricultural knowledge.

Our Mission

Krishi Mitra Assam is a digital platform designed to provide organized and easy-to-understand agricultural information for farmers.

Our goal is to help farmers learn about crops, cultivation methods, crop problems, modern farming techniques and agricultural resources.
